11-year revenue concentration · PT Andaru Persada Mandiri 2026 analysis · 2013–2023 ledger · 58.5% held by 24 accounts (2013–2017)

Ledger reconciliationYoY decompositionConcentration analysis

Problem. Fragmented order ledgers made volatile sales and customer dependence hard to quantify. Approach. Reconciled the 2013–2017 and 2018–2023 records, measured year-over-year change and grouped customers by contribution. Finding. In 2013–2017, 24 accounts held 58.5% of revenue; later annual growth ranged from +250% to −67%. The analysis supports a plan to protect core accounts, grow the middle tier and diversify the base.

Churn & forecasting · F&B case study 2024 · 2,430 transactions · 29% churn

RFMK-MeansCLTVProphet

Problem. Transaction records did not show how many customers stopped returning. Approach. Defined churn from observed repurchase cadence, used RFM, K-Means and CLTV to study customer value, then forecast the monthly series with Prophet. Finding. Across 2,430 transactions, churn was 29% and average customer lifetime was 3.45 months, pointing to retention as the priority. This case study does not report a measured retention lift.

SELECT a.nama, a.rt, a.rw, a.tps, b.kontak
FROM daftar_pemilih a
JOIN data_tim b
  ON similarity(a.nama, b.nama) > 0.55
  AND a.rt = b.rt AND a.tps = b.tps;

Voter database matching for a 2024 campaign 993 records · days → minutes

PostgreSQLpg_trgmRecord linkage

Problem. The campaign team spent days matching two voter lists by eye. Approach. Used PostgreSQL exact joins on normalized names and polling stations, then pg_trgm similarity at 0.55 for unmatched records. Result. Matched and exported 993 records across six neighborhoods in minutes, giving field coordinators a sorted list for follow-up.
